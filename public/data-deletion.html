How to remove your Hustlempires account and everything linked to it

To delete your account and data

  1. Email africazidisha@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Hustlempires account.
  2. Tell us your username. Send the request from the email address on your account so we know it is you.
  3. We delete the account within 30 days and email you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account details: username, password hash, email address and character details.
  • Your saved game, past lives, events, achievements, leaderboard scores, friends, gifts and market listings.
  • Your notification subscriptions and sign-in sessions.
  • The link between your account and your visit records.

What may be kept

Records of tips you paid can be kept where tax or payment law requires it, without your game data. Counts that do not identify you (such as the number of players who joined from an ad) stay in our reports.

Facebook and Instagram

Hustlempires does not use Facebook Login and does not receive your Facebook or Instagram account data, so there is nothing to remove there. If you used a Facebook or Instagram link to reach the game, that link only told us which post or ad you came from.
