HustlempiresPrivacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026 · applies to the Hustlempires game at hustlempires.com

Hustlempires is a free business life game. This page explains what information the game keeps, why, and how you can see it or have it deleted. Questions: africazidisha@gmail.com.

What we keep

  • Your account: username, password (stored only as a salted hash, never in readable form), the name, company name, town, country and gender you choose for your character, your avatar colour, and your email address if you give one.
  • Your game: your saved game, progress, the events in your story, past lives, achievements, leaderboard scores, friends, gifts and anything you list in the in-game markets.
  • Visits: a random visitor code stored in your browser, how many times you visited, the type of device (phone, tablet or computer), the link or campaign that brought you, and whether you created an account. We use this to count how many people our posts and ads bring to the game.
  • Notifications: if you turn on notifications, the push address your browser gives us and your time zone, so we can send a few game reminders (never at night).
  • Tips: if you tip the developers, the payment reference, amount, currency and status. Card and mobile money details are handled by our payment provider, Pesapal; we never see or store them.
  • Sign-in cookie: a session cookie keeps you logged in. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers in the game.

How we use it

  • To run the game: save your empire, show leaderboards, friends and markets, and let you log in on any device.
  • To send password-reset emails when you ask for one (sent through our email provider).
  • To understand which posts and ads bring players, using counts per link. We advertise on Facebook and Instagram and read the results Meta gives us (such as spend, reach and clicks per ad). We do not send your personal information to Meta or to advertisers, and we do not sell it to anyone.
  • To keep the game fair and safe, for example to stop cheating or abuse.

Who can see it

Other players can see what you show in the game: your character name, company, country, scores, leaderboard place and anything you list in the markets. Your email address, password and payment details are never shown to other players. Our hosting, email and payment providers process data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and game while your account exists. Visit records without an account are kept only as long as we need them for these counts. Records of tips can be kept longer where the law requires.

Your choices

  • You can play without giving an email address.
  • You can turn notifications off at any time in the game or in your browser.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account. See how to delete your data.

Children

Hustlempires is not made for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
