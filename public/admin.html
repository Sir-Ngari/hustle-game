    Events & challenges

    Every question the game asks players, grouped by category. Set how often random events happen, and make any category or single event rarer, commoner or switch it off. Changes reach players within a few minutes, the next time their game loads.

    Category · eventEvents · weightShare of random eventsShown, 30 daysShown, all timeSetting

    Shares are measured from simulated games: the chance that a random event is this one, for a typical player across a whole life. Events marked if only happen when their condition is met (owning a mine, being married, having children), shown as how much of a typical life they are possible. The Shown columns are what real players actually got. Events at 0% depend on choices simulated players rarely make (crime, sports teams, property deals) or on rare situations; real players will still see them. Billionaire challenges run on their own timer and are not in this draw.
